> **Automated GST invoice parsing and compliance reconciliation system** extracting structured financial entities from PDF/image invoices and verifying tax calculations against Indian GST slabs.

---

## 📌 Features & Compliance Checks

* **Invoice Field Extraction**: Extracts GSTIN, Invoice Date, HSN/SAC codes, CGST, SGST, IGST, Line Items, and Totals.
* **GSTIN Checksum Validator**: Algorithmic validation of GSTIN numbers to eliminate fraudulent invoice filings.
* **GSTR-2B Reconciliation**: Automated cross-matching between purchase invoices and GSTR tax portal filings.
